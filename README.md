## 15-Mark University Examination Structure

Every pathological condition in this monograph strictly adheres to the university 15-mark essay blueprint:
1. **Definition, WHO Classification & Epidemiology** (Age, Sex, Site predilection, Frequency)
2. **Histogenesis & Pathogenesis** (Dental lamina, cell rests of Serres/Malassez, mutations: PTCH1, BRAF V600E, TP53)
3. **Clinical Presentation** (Symptoms, Jaw expansion, cortical thinning, egg-shell crackling, paresthesia, root resorption)
4. **Radiographic Features** (Soap-bubble, tennis-racket, honeycomb, driven-snow, sunray, punched-out, cotton-wool)
5. **Detailed Histopathology** (Low-power architecture vs High-power cellular details; Vickers-Gorlin criteria, Rushton bodies, ghost cells, clear cells, amyloid stroma)
6. **Special Stains & Immunohistochemistry (IHC)** (Congo Red with apple-green birefringence, PAS, Calretinin, CK19, Ki-67)
7. **Structured Differential Diagnosis** (Clinical, Radiographic, and Histopathologic comparative matrices)
8. **Management & Surgical Considerations** (Enucleation, Curettage, Carnoy's solution, Resection & Recurrence rates)
9. **Syndromic Associations** (Gorlin-Goltz, Gardner, McCune-Albright, Maffucci, Sturge-Weber)
10. **Must-Draw Histological & Radiographic Schematics**

## Complete Syllabus Coverage (20 University Essays)

1. **Ameloblastoma & Histological Variants** (Conventional, Unicystic, Vickers-Gorlin criteria, follicular/plexiform/acanthomatous, resection margins)
2. **Benign Odontogenic Tumors** (CEOT/Pindborg tumor, Liesegang rings, AOT duct-like structures, Odontoma compound vs complex, Myxoma)
3. **Odontogenic Keratocyst (OKC / KCOT) & Gorlin-Goltz Syndrome** (PTCH1 mutation, 6-8 cell corrugated lining, picket-fence basal layer, satellite cysts, Carnoy's solution)
4. **Dentigerous Cyst & Inflammatory Odontogenic Cysts** (Reduced enamel epithelium, CEJ attachment, Radicular cyst, Rushton bodies, cholesterol clefts, foam cells)
5. **Non-Odontogenic & Developmental Cysts** (Nasopalatine duct cyst, Globulomaxillary, Dermoid & Epidermoid cysts, Branchial cleft cyst, Thyroglossal duct cyst)
6. **Dental Caries: Etiopathogenesis & Histopathology** (Keyes triad, Miller's chemico-parasitic theory, Stephan curve, 4 enamel zones, 5 dentinal zones, dead tracts)
7. **Developmental Disturbances of Tooth Structure** (Amelogenesis Imperfecta Witkop types 1-4, Dentinogenesis Imperfecta Shields 1-3, Dentin Dysplasia, Ghost teeth)
8. **Developmental Disturbances of Tooth Number, Size & Shape** (Anodontia, Mesiodens, Microdontia/Macrodontia, Gemination vs Fusion, Concrescence, Dens in dente, Taurodontism)
9. **Developmental Anomalies of Craniofacial Jaws & Soft Tissues** (Cleft lip & palate, Torus palatinus/mandibularis, Fordyce granules, Lingual thyroid, Fissured & Geographic tongue)
10. **Salivary Gland Neoplasms** (Pleomorphic Adenoma, Warthin's tumor, Mucoepidermoid Carcinoma AFIP grading, Adenoid Cystic Carcinoma perineural invasion)
11. **Non-Neoplastic Salivary Pathologies** (Extravasation vs Retention Mucocele, Ranula oral vs plunging, Sialolithiasis Wharton duct, Sjogren syndrome sicca complex, Necrotizing sialometaplasia)
12. **Oral Potentially Malignant Disorders: Leukoplakia & Erythroplakia** (WHO criteria, homogeneous vs non-homogeneous, PVL, 13 dysplasia criteria, carcinoma in situ)
13. **Oral Submucous Fibrosis (OSMF) & Oral Lichen Planus** (Areca nut arecoline/copper LOX pathogenesis, Pindborg stages, Wickham striae, saw-tooth pegs, Civatte bodies, band-like infiltrate)
14. **Oral Squamous Cell Carcinoma (OSCC)** (Tobacco/alcohol/HPV etiology, Broders histological grades 1-4, AJCC 8th Ed T-staging incorporating Depth of Invasion [DOI], neck nodal levels)
15. **Benign & Malignant Mesenchymal Soft Tissue Tumors** (Irritation fibroma, Pyogenic granuloma, PGCG cupping resorption, Schwannoma Antoni A/B & Verocay bodies, Neurofibromatosis Type 1)
16. **Oral Bacterial & Fungal Infections** (Oral Tuberculosis caseating granulomas, Syphilis chancre/mucous patch/gumma, Actinomycosis sulfur granules, Candidiasis pseudomembranous/erythematous)
17. **Oral Viral Infections** (HSV-1 primary herpetic gingivostomatitis, Tzanck smear, Herpes Zoster, EBV Oral Hairy Leukoplakia, HPV lesions)
18. **Vesiculobullous & Immunological Diseases** (Pemphigus vulgaris Desmoglein-3 suprabasal split vs Mucous Membrane Pemphigoid linear BMZ, Recurrent Aphthous stomatitis, Erythema Multiforme)
19. **Metabolic, Fibro-Osseous & Neoplastic Bone Diseases** (Fibrous Dysplasia GNAS mutation & Chinese letters, Paget's disease mosaic pattern & hypercementosis, CGCG, Multiple Myeloma, Osteosarcoma)
20. **Forensic Odontology & Identification Sciences** (Gustafson's age estimation 6 criteria, Suzuki & Tsuchihashi cheiloscopy, Lysell rugoscopy, Bite mark analysis ABFO scale)
