# Oral Pathology & Microbiology Master Study Suite

A comprehensive, interactive 15-mark university examination monograph, microscopic spotter laboratory, and clinical preparation platform for BDS (3rd Year) and Postgraduate Oral Pathology examinations based on Shafer, Neville, Cawson, Regezi, and OPN Lecture Blueprints.

## Core Features
* **15-Mark Essay Models**: All 20 conditions structured according to the 10-tier university examination standard.
* **Master PDF Monograph**: 70-page print-ready, copyable, and searchable PDF (`Oral_Pathology_Master_Notes.pdf`).
* **Interactive Dashboard**: Searchable, responsive web application (`index.html`) with live topic search, category filters, differential diagnosis matrices, and full essay views.
* **Microscopic Spotter Flashcards**: Interactive visual spotters covering pathognomonic criteria: Vickers-Gorlin sign (Ameloblastoma), Liesegang rings & apple-green birefringence (CEOT), OKC corrugated parakeratin, Chondromyxoid stroma (Pleomorphic Adenoma), Swiss-cheese cribriform pattern (Adenoid Cystic Carcinoma), Chinese letter woven bone (Fibrous Dysplasia), Mosaic bone pattern (Paget's), Saw-tooth rete pegs (Lichen Planus), Suprabasal Tzanck cells (Pemphigus), and Sulfur granules (Actinomycosis).
* **Diagnostic Calculators**: Interactive Gustafson's Dental Age Estimation Calculator ($Age = 11.43 + 4.56X$) and Broders Histological & DOI Staging Tool.
* **Viva Voce Quiz Engine**: High-yield university exam questions with instant feedback and explanatory pearls.

20. **Forensic Odontology & Identification Sciences** (Gustafson's age estimation 6 criteria, Suzuki & Tsuchihashi cheiloscopy, Lysell rugoscopy, Bite mark analysis ABFO scale)
