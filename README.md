## 15-Mark University Examination Structure

Every pathological condition in this monograph strictly adheres to the university 15-mark essay blueprint:
1. **Definition, WHO Classification & Epidemiology** (Age, Sex, Site predilection, Frequency)
2. **Histogenesis & Pathogenesis** (Dental lamina, cell rests of Serres/Malassez, mutations: PTCH1, BRAF V600E, TP53)
3. **Clinical Presentation** (Symptoms, Jaw expansion, cortical thinning, egg-shell crackling, paresthesia, root resorption)
4. **Radiographic Features** (Soap-bubble, tennis-racket, honeycomb, driven-snow, sunray, punched-out, cotton-wool)
5. **Detailed Histopathology** (Low-power architecture vs High-power cellular details; Vickers-Gorlin criteria, Rushton bodies, ghost cells, clear cells, amyloid stroma)
6. **Special Stains & Immunohistochemistry (IHC)** (Congo Red with apple-green birefringence, PAS, Calretinin, CK19, Ki-67)
7. **Structured Differential Diagnosis** (Clinical, Radiographic, and Histopathologic comparative matrices)
8. **Management & Surgical Considerations** (Enucleation, Curettage, Carnoy's solution, Resection & Recurrence rates)
9. **Syndromic Associations** (Gorlin-Goltz, Gardner, McCune-Albright, Maffucci, Sturge-Weber)
10. **Must-Draw Histological & Radiographic Schematics**

## Complete Syllabus Coverage (21 Chapters)

### Part I: Odontogenic Neoplasms & Cysts
- **Chapter 1**: Ameloblastoma & Histological Variants *(Conventional, Unicystic, Vickers-Gorlin criteria, follicular/plexiform/acanthomatous, resection margins)*
- **Chapter 2**: Benign Odontogenic Tumors: CEOT, AOT, Odontoma & Myxoma *(CEOT/Pindborg tumor, Liesegang rings, AOT duct-like structures, Odontoma compound vs complex, Myxoma)*
- **Chapter 3**: Odontogenic Keratocyst (OKC / KCOT) & Gorlin-Goltz Syndrome *(PTCH1 mutation, 6-8 cell corrugated lining, picket-fence basal layer, satellite cysts, Carnoy's solution)*
- **Chapter 4**: Dentigerous Cyst & Inflammatory Odontogenic Cysts *(Reduced enamel epithelium, CEJ attachment, Radicular cyst, Rushton bodies, cholesterol clefts, foam cells)*
- **Chapter 5**: Non-Odontogenic & Developmental Cysts of the Oral Region *(Nasopalatine duct cyst, Globulomaxillary, Dermoid & Epidermoid cysts, Branchial cleft cyst, Thyroglossal duct cyst)*

### Part II: Salivary Gland Pathologies, Premalignancy & Neoplasms
- **Chapter 6**: Salivary Gland Neoplasms: Pleomorphic Adenoma, Warthin's, MEC & AdCC *(Pleomorphic Adenoma, Warthin's tumor, Mucoepidermoid Carcinoma AFIP grading, Adenoid Cystic Carcinoma perineural invasion)*
- **Chapter 7**: Non-Neoplastic Salivary Pathologies: Mucocele, Ranula, Sialolithiasis & Sjogren's *(Extravasation vs Retention Mucocele, Ranula oral vs plunging, Sialolithiasis Wharton duct, Sjogren syndrome sicca complex, Necrotizing sialometaplasia)*
- **Chapter 8**: Oral Potentially Malignant Disorders (OPMD): Leukoplakia & Erythroplakia *(WHO criteria, homogeneous vs non-homogeneous, PVL, 13 dysplasia criteria, carcinoma in situ)*
- **Chapter 9**: Oral Submucous Fibrosis (OSMF) & Oral Lichen Planus *(Areca nut arecoline/copper LOX pathogenesis, Pindborg stages, Wickham striae, saw-tooth pegs, Civatte bodies, band-like infiltrate)*
- **Chapter 10**: Oral Squamous Cell Carcinoma (OSCC): Etiology, Broders Grading & TNM Staging *(Tobacco/alcohol/HPV etiology, Broders histological grades 1-4, AJCC 8th Ed T-staging incorporating Depth of Invasion [DOI], neck nodal levels)*
- **Chapter 11**: Benign & Malignant Mesenchymal Soft Tissue Tumors of the Oral Cavity *(Irritation fibroma, Pyogenic granuloma, PGCG cupping resorption, Schwannoma Antoni A/B & Verocay bodies, Neurofibromatosis Type 1)*

### Part III: Dental Caries & Developmental Disturbances
- **Chapter 12**: Dental Caries: Etiopathogenesis & Histopathology of Enamel & Dentin *(Keyes triad, Miller's chemico-parasitic theory, Stephan curve, 4 enamel zones, 5 dentinal zones, dead tracts)*
- **Chapter 13**: Developmental Disturbances of Tooth Structure: AI, DI & Dentin Dysplasia *(Amelogenesis Imperfecta Witkop types 1-4, Dentinogenesis Imperfecta Shields 1-3, Dentin Dysplasia, Ghost teeth)*
- **Chapter 14**: Developmental Disturbances of Tooth Number, Size & Shape *(Anodontia, Mesiodens, Microdontia/Macrodontia, Gemination vs Fusion, Concrescence, Dens in dente, Taurodontism)*
- **Chapter 15**: Developmental Anomalies of Craniofacial Jaws & Oral Soft Tissues *(Cleft lip & palate, Torus palatinus/mandibularis, Fordyce granules, Lingual thyroid, Fissured & Geographic tongue)*

### Part IV: Orofacial Infections, Bone Diseases & Forensic Odontology
- **Chapter 16**: Oral Bacterial & Fungal Infections: TB, Syphilis, Actinomycosis & Candidiasis *(Oral Tuberculosis caseating granulomas, Syphilis chancre/mucous patch/gumma, Actinomycosis sulfur granules, Candidiasis pseudomembranous/erythematous)*
- **Chapter 17**: Oral Viral Infections: HSV, VZV, EBV (Oral Hairy Leukoplakia) & HPV *(HSV-1 primary herpetic gingivostomatitis, Tzanck smear, Herpes Zoster, EBV Oral Hairy Leukoplakia, HPV lesions)*
- **Chapter 18**: Vesiculobullous & Immunological Diseases: Pemphigus, Pemphigoid, Aphthae & EM *(Pemphigus vulgaris Desmoglein-3 suprabasal split vs Mucous Membrane Pemphigoid linear BMZ, Recurrent Aphthous stomatitis, Erythema Multiforme)*
- **Chapter 19**: Metabolic, Fibro-Osseous & Neoplastic Bone Diseases of the Jaws *(Fibrous Dysplasia GNAS mutation & Chinese letters, Paget's disease mosaic pattern & hypercementosis, CGCG, Multiple Myeloma, Osteosarcoma)*
- **Chapter 20**: Forensic Odontology: Gustafson's Method, Cheiloscopy, Rugoscopy & Bite Marks *(Gustafson's age estimation 6 criteria, Suzuki & Tsuchihashi cheiloscopy, Lysell rugoscopy, Bite mark analysis ABFO scale)*

### Part V: Rapid Revision
- **Chapter 21**: Rapid Revision Master Cheat Sheet: Pathognomonic Histopathology Buzzwords, Cyst/Tumor Matrices & Syndromic Associations *(Pathognomonic buzzwords, differential diagnosis matrices, syndromic associations, and exam night pearls)*
